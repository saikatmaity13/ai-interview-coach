# AI Interview Coach - Demo Walkthrough

This script will guide you through recording a walkthrough video of the AI Interview Coach.

## Prerequisites
1. Open terminal and run: `uvicorn backend.app.main:app --reload`
2. Open another terminal and run: `streamlit run frontend/streamlit_app.py`
3. Copy a sample Job Description and Resume to your clipboard.

## Scene 1: Starting the Interview
- Point out the Streamlit app interface.
- Highlight the fields for Job Description and Resume.
- **Action**: Paste your sample JD and Resume into the respective fields.
- **Action**: Click "Start Interview".
- The screen will transition to the "Live Interview" layout, loading the first question generated by the `question_generator` node.

## Scene 2: The Interview Flow
- The coach will present the first question based on your JD/Resume.
- **Action**: Read the question, click the microphone widget to record your answer.
- **Action**: Speak a reasonably good technical answer (e.g. "I used FastAPI for the backend to achieve high concurrency...").
- Submit the audio.
- The UI will say "Processing answer...". The audio is sent via WebSocket, transcribed by `faster-whisper`, evaluated by `answer_evaluator`, and the next state is generated.
- Observe the Coach's live encouraging feedback and the next question appearing on screen.

## Scene 3: Triggering a Follow-up
- **Action**: For the next question, give an intentionally brief or superficial answer (e.g. "I just wrote some Python scripts and it worked").
- The `answer_evaluator` node will detect a lack of depth and set `needs_followup: True`.
- The `followup_agent` will then generate a probing question on the same topic (e.g., "Could you elaborate on how you structured those scripts?").

## Scene 4: Dashboard & Summary
- Continue answering until the interview concludes (set to a short 4-question loop for the demo).
- Once the interview ends, the screen will switch to the **Post-Session Dashboard**.
- **Action**: Show the Plotly radar chart, explaining how it visualizes performance across categories (Technical vs Behavioral) and dimensions (Correctness, Depth, Clarity).
- Scroll down and expand the detailed reviews for each question to show the specific 1-5 scores and justifications generated by the evaluator agent.
